# Settle-loop yield on a fabrichat room: repeat on a quiet machine

Measured 2026-10-14, 19:16–20:26 UTC. Arm `a` is `05047f25b2` (pull request
8782's head) plus this harness; arm `b` is `e2b27bfdae`, the merge base with
`origin/main`. The two arms differ only in `packages/runner` (scheduler
`settle.ts`, `cooperative-yield.ts`, `execution.ts`, `facade.ts`, `runtime.ts`,
`executor/space-server.ts`), plus tests and docs.

## Verdict

Arm `a` is the branch, with the yield; arm `b` is `main`, without it.

**The branch does not make the room load faster.** Its one detectable cost is
about 1.2 s, or 12%, on the settled view in the browser.

- **Where the slowdown comes from:** arm `a`'s settle span includes the turns it
  hands back, about 142 per load totaling 1.64 s.
  - With those removed, `a`'s own settle work is 0.9 s shorter than `b`'s.
  - Action run time is equal.
  - So the extra settle time is spent waiting in turns, not doing extra work.
- **Deno CLI reloads:** no difference in wall time (27.5 s against 28.0 s) or
  CPU time (32.5 s against 33.0 s).
- **Receive silence during the settle:**
  - Arm `b` reads nothing for its whole 6–8 s settle.
  - Arm `a` reads 500 or more frames during its settle.
  - Arm `a` still has a 3.4–4.8 s silence, while one action waits on a single
    watch reply.
  - The silences before the settle are the same in both arms.

## Deviations from the README's steps

1. **The browser.** Two changes, both needed before a browser load could run at
   all:
   - Chrome refuses to start as root without `--no-sandbox`
     (`zygote_host_impl_linux.cc: Running as root without --no-sandbox is not
     supported`).
     `docs/development/TESTING.md` says not to add that flag, so `browser.sh`
     ran as a non-root user, `perf`, with Chrome's sandbox intact. User
     namespaces are enabled here.
   - With no Chrome at the system paths `astral-adapter.ts` searches, Astral
     downloaded its built-in Chromium 125. Under it the runtime worker fails at
     start
     (`[RuntimeWorker] Error: TypeError: Error.isError is not a
     function`),
     and the room's messages never appear, so `waitForText` gave up after 5
     minutes and no summary was written. The browser loads therefore ran with
     `ASTRAL_BIN_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome`
     (Chromium 141.0.7390.37, preinstalled here). That is the same override CI
     uses to point Astral at the runner's own Chrome.

   The scripts themselves are unchanged. The failed Chromium 125 attempts (one
   `w1` pair and two diagnostic loads) were deleted before the 24 pairs ran. All
   48 loads below used Chromium 141.
2. **`column` is not installed**, so `render-table.py` and the per-load rows of
   `browser-table.py` were aligned with a small Python formatter instead of
   `column -t`. The numbers are unchanged.
3. **Server stats were read twice:** once at 19:40, after the Deno renders and
   before any browser load succeeded (arm `a` only), and once at 20:24, at the
   end of the session (both arms).

## Reproducing the derived numbers

The scripts beside this report read `$SETTLE_YIELD_OUT/out`. They take the same
inputs as `browser-table.py` and `settle-silence.py`, and align the clocks the
same way, on the first `session.watch.add` frame and its `watchAdd/request`
measure.

- `stats.py <out-dir>` gives the confidence intervals, the cell-latency split,
  the settle-minus-turns comparison, and wall and CPU per reload.
- `silences.py <out-dir> <label>...` gives each render's three longest silences,
  with what was sent in them and the `start/*` spans they overlap.
- `inside.py <out-dir> <label>...` shows what the client did inside the longest
  silence within the settle.
- `align.py` stands in for `column -t -s$'\t'`.
